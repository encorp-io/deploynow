# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions
about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by contacting [encorp.ai](https://encorp.ai). Maintainers may remove content
and block people who break these rules.
